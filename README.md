## Marketing-Campaign-Performance-Analytics
Data Analytics Project | PostgreSQL | Python | Power BI | DAX | Marketing Analytics

---

### Project Overview

Digital marketing campaigns generate large volumes of performance data across campaigns, platforms, regions, and content types. Extracting actionable insights from this data is essential for improving campaign performance, optimizing advertising spend, and maximizing marketing return.

This project analyzes campaign performance using **PostgreSQL** for business analysis, **Python** for exploratory and statistical analysis, and **Power BI** to develop an executive dashboard that supports data-driven marketing decisions.

---

### Business Problem

Marketing teams invest advertising budgets across multiple campaigns, platforms, regions, and content types. However, identifying which marketing activities generate the highest return, which campaigns require optimization, and where budgets should be allocated efficiently becomes increasingly challenging as campaign volume grows.

Management wants to answer the following business questions:

- Which campaigns generate the highest revenue?
- Which campaigns deliver the highest ROAS?
- Which campaigns have the lowest CPA?
- Which marketing platforms perform best?
- Which regions generate the highest return?
- Which content types convert users most effectively?
- Where should marketing budgets be increased or optimized?

---

### Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall marketing campaign performance.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming campaigns.
- Compare platform performance using marketing KPIs.
- Analyze regional and content-type effectiveness.
- Measure campaign efficiency using ROAS, CPA, CTR, and Conversion Rate.
- Deliver actionable business recommendations for marketing optimization.

### Key Insights

- **North America–Carousel** campaigns generated the highest **ROAS (12.01)**, delivering the strongest return on advertising spend.
- **Asia–Image** campaigns achieved the **lowest CPA (9.64)** and the **highest Conversion Rate (65.8%)**, making them the most efficient campaign combination.
- **Video** content consistently demonstrated strong conversion performance across multiple regions.
- **Africa–Text** campaigns recorded the **lowest ROAS (6.19)** alongside one of the highest acquisition costs, indicating significant optimization opportunities.
- Marketing effectiveness varied across **Region–Content** combinations, highlighting the importance of multidimensional campaign analysis.

---

### Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing **North America–Carousel** campaigns while monitoring performance.
- Scale **Asia–Image** campaigns due to their high efficiency and conversion performance.
- Expand the use of **Video** creatives across additional regions.
- Optimize **Africa–Text** campaigns by improving audience targeting and creative strategy.
- Prioritize marketing budget allocation using **Region–Content performance** rather than regional performance alone.

---

### Business Value Delivered

- Identified high-performing campaigns, platforms, and regions to support marketing budget allocation.
- Evaluated campaign efficiency using ROAS, CPA, CTR, and Conversion Rate.
- Combined regional and content-type analysis to uncover optimization opportunities not visible through single-dimension analysis.
- Delivered an executive-ready Power BI dashboard for monitoring marketing performance.
- Translated analytical findings into actionable business recommendations to improve marketing efficiency.
